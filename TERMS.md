## Music

The public playlist is configured in the repository and may be heard by all
visitors. Add only audio that you own or have permission to publish and make
available.

Music files selected by a visitor and audio URLs they save are kept in that
visitor's browser on that device. The site does not upload that private library
or provide cross-device synchronization. URLs are fetched by the visitor's
browser from their audio host when played. Visitors are responsible for the
rights and terms that apply to their files and URLs.

Playback position is saved locally when supported. Browsers may block autoplay,
so after a reload the visitor may need to press Play to resume.
